2. Description of Service

SignalVeil is a health-data noise-reduction and translation app. It reads only 8 core health metrics, distinguishes signal from noise, arbitrates conflicts between device scores and how you feel, and uses AI to translate the result into plain-language daily briefs. Key features include:

  • Daily brief: a one-paragraph summary each morning of the health changes most worth your attention.
  • Signal / noise classification: 7-day and 30-day trends only, automatically filtering single-day random fluctuation.
  • Conflict arbitration: when a device score disagrees with how you feel, we trust how you feel.
  • AI Q&A: ask questions about your health data in natural language (Pro feature).
  • Weekly deep report: a review of the week's trends every Sunday (Pro feature).
